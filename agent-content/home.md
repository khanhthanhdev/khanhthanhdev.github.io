# Tran Khanh Thanh

Tran Khanh Thanh (Trần Khánh Thành, also known as Thanh Tran and
khanhthanhdev) is an Electrical and Computer Engineering student at
VinUniversity in Hanoi, Vietnam. This site documents his work in AI agents,
robotics, simulation, and reliable full-stack systems.

## Current focus

His current work explores how AI agents can make traditional processes more
reliable and faster, with particular attention to robotics workflows that must
operate outside a notebook. Areas of interest include agent evaluation,
teleoperation-compatible robot software, MuJoCo simulation, reproducible data
pipelines, and the engineering needed to connect those systems.

## Experience and recognition

At [STEAM for Vietnam](https://steamforvietnam.org/), Thanh contributed to
software for robotics tournament operations. He received First Prize in the
[Vietnam AI Contest 2024](https://vlabinnovation.com/tin-tuc-su-kien/chinh-thuc-mo-cong-dang-ky-vietnam-ai-contest-2024/)
and a Gold Medal at the [FIRST Global Challenge 2023](https://first.global/).
These experiences shape his interest in robotics systems that are dependable
for collaborators and users.
