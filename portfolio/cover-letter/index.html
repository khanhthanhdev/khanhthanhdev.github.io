Cover Letter

VinRobotics Talent Program Internship application letter.

VinRobotics Talent Program Hiring Team,

I am an Electrical and Computer Engineering student at VinUniversity applying for the VinRobotics Talent Program Internship. I am seeking a research-engineering role centered on reinforcement learning, imitation learning, and learning from demonstration, with teleoperation and simulation as supporting tools for collecting, testing, and understanding robot behavior. I am especially interested in VinRobotics because the Physical Intelligence Lab’s Embodied ML direction connects research ideas to validation in both simulation and real hardware.

My most relevant work is a contribution to an existing LeRobot/Hiwonder NexArm fork. I helped make its robot-learning loop more inspectable and reproducible: a MuJoCo simulation preserves the six-action NexArm interface; front and wrist cameras support observation; scripted pick-and-place demonstrations generate datasets with provenance; and ACT-compatible rollout and benchmark paths support policy evaluation. I also integrated Rerun-based inspection and focused validation around raw-action conversion, camera outputs, gripper direction, collision geometry, and task behavior. After correcting an oversized invisible gripper collider, a deterministic scripted pick-and-place test succeeded on 10 of 10 seeds. I describe that accurately as scripted simulation evidence, not learned-policy performance.

PRISM, a multi-dimensional benchmark for AI peer reviewers, gave me complementary research experience. I contributed an evidence-grounded validation workflow with explicit dataset contracts, blinded annotation tooling, cached judge inputs, and statistical reporting. It trained me to identify what an experiment can support, keep its inputs traceable, and report limitations rather than smoothing them away. I would bring that same approach to demonstrations, policy rollouts, and sim-to-real experiments at VinRobotics.

At STEAM for Vietnam, I have built software for robotics competition operations, where shared state, local-network reliability, scheduling, and rule processing have to work for a team under event pressure. Earlier, competing at FIRST Global Challenge 2023 made robotics a long-term commitment rather than a recent technical interest. Together, these experiences have shaped how I work with collaborators: make interfaces explicit, test failure cases, and leave others enough evidence to continue the work.

I would value the opportunity to learn from VinRobotics researchers while contributing practical infrastructure for RL, imitation learning, and LfD experiments. I am ready to bring Python, C++, PyTorch, LeRobot, MuJoCo, testing, debugging, and technical English to a team that is serious about making embodied learning results hold up beyond a single demo.

Sincerely, Tran Khanh Thanh

Revision record

This letter was revised to keep the employer-specific case separate from the reflective statement of purpose. It opens with the intended internship scope, then maps the NexArm contribution to demonstration collection, simulation, debugging, and policy evaluation in language a hiring team can assess quickly. PRISM, STEAM for Vietnam, and FIRST Global each serve a distinct purpose: research method, collaboration under operational constraints, and sustained robotics motivation. The final paragraph names the practical skills I would bring to an embodied-ML team and avoids claims about policy performance that the available evidence does not support.

Evidence used: Physical Intelligence Lab, NexArm repository, and PRISM.
