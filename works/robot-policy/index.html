Robot policies on the NexArm

Interactive walkthroughs of the policies we train for the 6-DOF Hiwonder NexArm, each shown step by step next to a 3D model of the arm stacking bowls.

Each page follows one control cycle of a policy: what the cameras and joint encoders deliver, how the network turns them into tokens, and how many actions it commits to before looking again. The 3D view is the NexArm from our MuJoCo model, placed in the bowl-stacking workcell with the same front, top and wrist cameras the policies are trained on. The project background is in Building a Robot Learning Pipeline for the 6-DOF NexArm.

Action Chunking with Transformers

ACT

A CVAE-trained transformer that turns the NexArm's camera frames and joint state into 100 future joint targets, then runs all of them before it looks again.

  • 100 · actions per chunk
  • 100 · actions run before re-planning
  • 30 Hz · control rate
  • 902 · encoder tokens (3 cameras)
TurboVLA, a compact vision-language-action model

TurboVLA

DINOv3 image tokens and a BERT-encoded instruction are fused by six bi-attention layers, and an ACT-style decoder predicts 16 actions, of which the NexArm runs 8 before looking again.

  • 16 · actions per chunk
  • 8 · actions run before re-planning
  • 30 Hz · control rate
  • 588 · vision tokens (3 cameras)
B-spline Policy, ACT with a B-spline action head

BSP

The ACT network with a different output. Instead of 16 actions it predicts 16 knots and control points of a cubic B-spline, which the controller samples at 100 Hz and can run several times faster.

  • 16 · knots per segment
  • cubic · spline degree
  • 10 Hz · policy rate
  • 100 Hz · control rate
