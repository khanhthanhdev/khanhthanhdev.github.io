Robot Policy Explorer

Step-by-step walkthroughs of the policies we train for the 6-DOF NexArm, each next to a 3D model of the arm.

Open the explorer Compare all 3 policies: what each one sees, how it tokenizes, and how many actions it runs before looking again.
ACT

Action Chunking with Transformers

A CVAE-trained transformer that turns the NexArm's camera frames and joint state into 100 future joint targets, then runs all of them before it looks again.

  • 100 actions per chunk
  • 100 actions run before re-planning
Walk through it
TurboVLA

TurboVLA, a compact vision-language-action model

DINOv3 image tokens and a BERT-encoded instruction are fused by six bi-attention layers, and an ACT-style decoder predicts 16 actions, of which the NexArm runs 8 before looking again.

  • 16 actions per chunk
  • 8 actions run before re-planning
Walk through it
BSP

B-spline Policy, Diffusion Policy with B-spline actions

A Diffusion Policy that denoises the knots and control points of a cubic B-spline instead of a list of actions; the controller samples the curve at 100 Hz and can run it several times faster.

  • 16 knots per segment
  • cubic spline degree
Walk through it
