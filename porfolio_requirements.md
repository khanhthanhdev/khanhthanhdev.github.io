## Online Portfolio Design & Presentation

### Platform Setup – Required Elements

1. **Home / Landing Page**

- Your name
- Brief professional tagline or introduction
- Navigation to all portfolio sections
- Professional photo _(optional, but recommended)_

2. **Statement of Purpose Page**

- Featured as the first artifact/section
- Easy to locate and read

3. **Professional Document Pages**

- Separate page or clearly labeled section for each document
- Includes the final document
- Includes revision narrative and evidence of process
- Includes peer feedback documentation

4. **Contact / About Section** _(optional)_

- Professional email
- LinkedIn profile _(if applicable)_
- Other relevant professional links

## Design Principles

### Professional Appearance

- Clean, uncluttered layout
- Consistent color scheme and fonts
- Adequate white space
- Easy-to-read font sizes _(minimum 11–12pt for body text)_

### User Experience

- Clear, intuitive navigation
- Functional links _(all buttons and menus work)_
- Mobile-responsive design _(readable on phone/tablet)_
- Fast loading times _(optimize images)_

### Accessibility

- Sufficient color contrast for readability
- Alt text for images
- Readable fonts _(avoid overly decorative choices)_
- Logical heading hierarchy

### Professionalism

- No spelling or grammatical errors in website text
- Professional language throughout
- Appropriate images _(if used)_
- Working contact links
